### 6. Technical Architecture & Animation Requirements
- Tech Stack: Next.js (App Router), React, Tailwind CSS, TypeScript.
- Animation & Physics: Framer Motion for card stacking, scroll reveals, and tab transitions.
- Lens Distortion Shader: WebGL / Three.js / Canvas fragment shader with chromatic dispersion to create the liquid glass magnifying lens effect.
- Responsiveness: Fully adaptive layout from mobile screens to 4K ultra-wide displays with touch-friendly card navigation.
